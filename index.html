Who We Are

Erawat Pharma Limited, an Erawat Group Company, ranks among the top Capsule manufacturers in the world & second largest in India.

Manufacturing empty hard capsules since 1997, our team brings more than 30 years of total group experience in capsule manufacturing technologies.
