Packaging Integrity

Sealed liners built for sea and air freight.

Capsules are packed in sealed heavy-gauge food-grade polyethylene liners inside heavy-duty cartons, built to travel by sea or air and arrive strictly within dimensional and moisture specification.

Reefer containers with digital data-loggers are deployed during warm-weather ocean routes to ensure storage temperatures never exceed 25°C.
