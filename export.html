One Star Export House

Delivering zero-defect capsules across 40+ countries.

From our WHO-cGMP automated cleanrooms in Central India, Erawat Pharma Limited exports over 12 billion empty hard Gelatin and HPMC vegetarian capsules annually to pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, and contract manufacturing partners worldwide — including regulated markets across Europe and the United States.

Recognized by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) as a One Star Export House, our dedicated export desk manages documentation, customs clearances, moisture-barrier export packaging, and dependable air and sea freight schedules.

Regulatory & Trade Desk

Audit-ready export documentation on every shipment.

Smooth customs clearance and regulatory registration are critical to uninterrupted capsule supply. Our export documentation team prepares complete shipping and technical packets before dispatch.

  • Batch Quality Packet: Lot-specific Certificate of Analysis (COA), microbiological test summary, and pharmacopeial compliance declaration.
  • Customs & Banking Set: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Airway Bill / Bill of Lading, and Export Letter of Credit (LC) compliance.
  • Origin & Diet Compliance: TSE/BSE-Free certificates, JUHF Halal, Kosher supervision letters, and US FDA DMF Type IV reference access.
  • Direct Export Desk: Dedicated coordination via marketing@erawatgroup.com and +91 731 6659100.

Sea & Air Freight Packaging

Engineered to preserve moisture & shell integrity in transit.
