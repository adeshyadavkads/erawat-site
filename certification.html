What this means for your audit

Batch Integrity & Traceability

  • Traceable lots: Every raw material delivery and final shipping carton is linked to a permanent batch manufacturing record.
  • Retention samples: Maintained in environmental stability chambers for the full shelf life of each batch.
  • Change control: Formal procedural review before any engineering or formulation adjustment is implemented.

Testing & Personnel Control

  • Pharmacopeial test methods: Validated against IP, BP, and USP standards for disintegration, moisture, and bioburden.
  • Controlled storage: Cleanrooms and packaging zones operate under continuous computerized humidity and temperature logging.
  • Documented training: Rigorous standard operating procedure (SOP) qualification for line operators and QA analysts.
