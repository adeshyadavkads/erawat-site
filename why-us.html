Operational Reliability

Protecting your line efficiency shift after shift.

Capsules are a small cost in your product and a big cost when they fail. This is how we keep your line running.

01

Fewer rejections.

Minimal rejections on the filling machine mean higher yield and less wasted formulation. Our consistent dome curvature and wall thickness prevent crushing, telescoping, and split ends.

What this means for you: Less expensive fill material thrown away.
Filling machine close-up, 4:3
02

Technical help when you need it.

Our technical team and R&D unit are available to fix mechanical and setting problems on your filling machines. When machine tolerances drift or powder density shifts, our engineers provide prompt tooling adjustments.

What this means for you: You call us, not wait for a report.
Engineer at machine, 4:3
03

Less downtime.

Capsule performance is built to give minimum downtime on the filling machine, so machine and operator output go up. Smooth separation and positive locking reduce cleaning stops.

What this means for you: More capsules filled per shift.
Packed bottles, 4:3
