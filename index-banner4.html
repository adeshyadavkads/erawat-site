Who We Are

Erawat Pharma Limited, an Erawat Group Company, ranks among the top Capsule manufacturers in the world & second largest in India.

Manufacturing empty hard capsules since 1997, our team brings more than 30 years of total group experience in capsule manufacturing technologies. Backed by high technical competence and rigorous R & D, we manufacture empty Gelatin Capsules as well as plant-derived HPMC Capsules for vegetarian and hygroscopic formulations.
